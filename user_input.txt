Introduction
Nkangala District Municipality (NDM) is a Category C Municipality, vested with executive and legislative authority over the Nkangala region, which comprises multiple Local Municipalities under the jurisdiction of the District Council, 

NDM has functions provided for in terms of Section 84 of the Municipal Structures Act 117 of 1998, which provides the functions of a District Municipality as follows:

•	Integrated Development-Planning for the district municipality as a whole, including a framework for integrated development plans (for the local municipalities within) of all municipalities in the area of the district municipality, taking into account the Integrated Development Plans of those local municipalities)
•	(Bulk Supply of water that affects a significant proportion of municipalities in the district) Potable Water Supply Systems.
•	Bulk supply of electricity, which includes for the purpose of such supply, the transmission, distribution and, where applicable, regeneration of electricity
•	Bulk Sewer Purification Works and main sewage disposal that affects a significant proportion of municipalities in the district) Domestic Waste Water and Sewage Disposal Systems.
•	Solid waste disposal sites, in so far as relates to the establishment, operation and control of waste disposal sites, bulk waste transfer facilities and waste disposal; facilities for more than one local Municipality in the District.
•	Municipal roads which form an integral part of a road transport system for the area of the district municipality as a whole”

Having been appointed by the Department of Department of Water and Sanitation (DWS) as an Implementation Agent (IA) for the Regional Bulk Infrastructure Grant (RBIG) and the Water Services Infrastructure Grant (WSIG) in selected Local Municipalities within its jurisdiction, NDM has demonstrated a huge capacity and ability in the implementation of the large-scale projects. 

While NDM is not directly responsible for the delivery of basic services, such as water, sanitation, electricity, roads and stormwater, and community services due to these functions being primarily carried out by the Local Municipalities, NDM has always been providing an additional support function wherein it augments the limited resources being made available by the local municipalities through implementation of additional infrastructure investment projects on behalf of Local Municipalities.  

This support forms part of broader efforts to build capacity, promote infrastructure development, and address service delivery backlogs across all six Local Municipalities, The Technical Services Department is responsible for managing the implementation of these infrastructure capital projects.





Component A:  Basic Services
This component encompasses key infrastructure services, including water supply, wastewater (sanitation), electricity, and roads and stormwater management. Access to improved sanitation also increased, with 63,2% of households benefiting in 2020 compared too 60.4% in 2016. In Mpumalanga, approximately 193,081 households still lacked access to piped water in any form, while 99,563 households continued to reside in informal dwellings. Additionally, 111,377 households were not connected to electricity, and 514,554 households did not have access to a hygienic toilet. Furthermore, 881,146 households reported that they did not receive municipal refuse removal services at least once a week or less frequently. 
In 2021, only 63.2% of households in Mpumalanga had access to improved sanitation, making it the second lowest among South Africa’s nine provinces. As shown in Table 3.1, Mpumalanga also had the second highest proportion of households using unhygienic toilets (36.8%), according to the Socio-Economic Review and Outlook (SERO) for Mpumalanga, March 2023.

3.1 Water provision 

NDM has made significant investments in Water infrastructure in the entire Nkangala District over the past few years. With the construction of Loskop Bulk Water Supply at an advanced stage, the intended benefiting communities of Thembisile Hani Local Municipality and nearby villages in Elias Motsoaledi Local Municipality will have so much relieve after such a long time of drought.  

Furthermore, Implementation Readiness Study (IRS) for Western Highveld (Rust de Winter) Bulk Water Supply has since been approved by the Department of Water and Sanitation (DWS).  NDM with the support from DWS made an application to Infrastructure South Africa (ISA) for assistance with project readiness to submit to Budget Fund Infrastructure (BFI).  Western Highveld (Rust de Winter) Bulk Water Supply will provide sustainable water to thirteen villages (Masobe, Katjibane, Loding, Marapyane, Nokaneng Mantiole, Mmaduma, Mmamethlake, Phake, Ramantsho, Rankaile, Seabe and Semohlase villages) that are falling within 10 wards in Dr JS Moroka. 

NDM has further been appointed as Implementing Agent for implementation of the Emalahleni Bulk Infrastructure Conditional Assessment Project under RBIG. Construction will commence on the 2025/2026 Financial Year.
NDM has further procure borehole material for Dr JS Moroka municipality to enable the repair and maintenance of the existing boreholes which are no longer functional due to the requiring repairs and material replacements in certain instances.
